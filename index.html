Sensor fusion · Inertial navigation · Bayesian filtering传感器融合 · 惯性导航 · 贝叶斯滤波

Estimating where people and devices are when the outside world offers few observations. 在外部观测稀少的条件下，估计人与设备的运动状态。

I am a doctoral candidate in Automation at Aalto University, supervised by Professor Simo Särkkä (ELLIS Fellow), and expect to receive my doctoral degree in December 2026. My research focuses on intelligent sensing and multisensor information fusion, particularly probabilistic state estimation for complex dynamic systems with limited external observations. My work has appeared in IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement, IEEE Sensors Journal and leading international conferences.

芬兰阿尔托大学自动化专业博士生，师从贝叶斯滤波与状态估计领域知名学者、欧洲机器学习与智能系统实验室会士（ELLIS Fellow）Simo Särkkä 教授，将于 2026 年 12 月获得博士学位。主要从事智能感知与多传感器信息融合研究，聚焦于复杂动态系统中弱外部观测条件下的概率状态估计。相关成果发表于 IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement、IEEE Sensors Journal 等国际期刊及重要国际会议。

Research研究方向

Wearable IMU

Human kinematic constraints人体运动学约束

Modeling inter-foot distance, spacing vectors and gait symmetry as constraints that bound drift in foot-mounted inertial positioning.

将双脚间距、间距向量与步态对称性建模为约束，抑制足部惯性定位中的累积漂移。

Differentiable filtering

Learning filter parameters可微滤波参数估计

Optimizing Kalman filter parameters with automatic differentiation from sparse, unordered and probabilistic supervision.

基于自动微分，在稀疏、无序、概率性监督信息下优化卡尔曼滤波器参数。

Optical tracking

Smartphone motion capture智能手机光学运动跟踪

Open-source multicamera tracking with consumer phones, and extrinsic calibration from noisy distances between random spatial markers.

基于消费级手机的开源多相机跟踪系统，以及利用随机空间标记点间含噪距离的相机外参标定。
